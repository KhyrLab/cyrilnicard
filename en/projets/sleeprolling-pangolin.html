SleepRolling Pangolin

A puzzle game where you guide a sleeping pangolin to the finish by placing tiles that change its movement.

Play on itch.io ↗

The concept

The pangolin moves while asleep and the player does not control it directly. You place different tiles in the level to change its path and guide it to the finish tile.

A project born during Ludum Dare

The initial concept was built in 72 hours with Daepso for Ludum Dare 46. The project then continued to evolve with new levels and several interface improvements.

The game

The published version includes 26 levels plus 3 large bonus levels. It can be played directly in the browser through a WebGL build, with Windows, macOS and Linux versions also available on itch.io.

Contributions

The project was made with Daepso. Nytrouss and CZG helped design some levels, Raphael Samakh drew the pangolin and Kekours created the music.
