PROJECT

Geometric origami

An exploration of complex geometric forms in paper.

Discovery

I discovered this form of geometric origami through a Micmath video. I am especially interested in regular shapes and stellated polyhedra.

Favourite forms

The great stellated dodecahedron is one of my favourite models. The small stellated dodecahedron is also a striking shape — and is reminiscent of the stars used in Super Mario Galaxy.
