PROJECT

Papercraft

From a digital model to a reinforced paper object.

The idea

Papercraft turns a digital model into a paper template to cut, fold and assemble. I used Pepakura to prepare the templates.

Assembly

Once the shape is assembled, I reinforce it with epoxy resin and, when needed, fiberglass. The piece can then be sanded and painted with acrylic paint.

One of the archived projects is a Cubone model made with this method.
