PROJECT

3D printing

A project focused on designing and printing 3D parts.

The project

I use a Dagoma DiscoEasy200 3D printer to make useful — or simply fun — objects such as game pieces, small accessories and furniture improvements.

Design

Most modelling was done in Fusion 360, with Blender for more specific needs. The goal is not only to obtain the final part but also to understand the process: design, manufacturing constraints, testing and iteration.

Printing

The previous version of the site also linked to several models shared on Thingiverse under the username Khyrgrim, with notes about print settings.
