Coconut & apricot dahl

A red lentil dahl with roasted squash, coconut cream and dried apricots.

Plant-based recipePersonal archive

Ingredients

  • 1 butternut squash
  • 400 g red lentils
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • A piece of ginger, about 5 cm
  • 100 g dried apricots
  • 250 g coconut cream
  • About 150 ml olive oil
  • 1.5 L vegetable stock
  • 3 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp turmeric
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh coriander and cumin/coriander/sunflower/pumpkin seeds (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 190 °C. Cut the butternut squash into roughly 1 cm cubes.
  2. Toss the cubes with about 50 ml olive oil, leave for 20 minutes, then roast for about 30 minutes.
  3. Finely slice the onion and garlic, then grate the ginger. Fry the garlic and ginger in about 50 ml oil for 1 minute over medium heat.
  4. Optionally add coriander and cumin seeds, then add the onion and let it begin to brown.
  5. Add the lentils, coconut cream, stock, cumin and turmeric.
  6. Once boiling, reduce the heat, cover and cook for about 40 minutes, stirring regularly.
  7. Five minutes before the end, add the roasted squash and dried apricots cut into small pieces.
  8. Optional: finely blend fresh coriander with a little olive oil and drizzle it over the dahl when serving.

Notes

For a thicker dahl, reduce the amount of stock. Sunflower or pumpkin seeds added at the end bring extra crunch.
