Chocolate & speculoos tart

A rich tart made with a speculoos base and chocolate ganache.

Plant-based recipePersonal archive

Ingredients

  • 400 g speculoos biscuits
  • 5 tbsp margarine
  • 200 g dark baking chocolate
  • 90 g coconut, hazelnut or walnut oil
  • 90 g icing sugar
  • 100 ml plant-based cream (coconut or soy)
  • 1 sachet vanilla sugar (optional)
  • 1 tbsp rum or Grand Marnier (optional)
  • 50 g ground almonds (optional)
  • Pear, apple or raspberry (optional)

Method

  1. Finely crush the speculoos biscuits, add the margarine and mix until evenly combined.
  2. Press the mixture into a tart tin, forming a rim, then bake for about 25 minutes at 190 °C. Leave to cool.
  3. Melt the chocolate. Add the oil, then the icing sugar and, optionally, vanilla sugar.
  4. Add the plant-based cream, then optionally the ground almonds and alcohol. Mix well.
  5. Pour the ganache over the tart base. Add fruit on top if desired.
  6. Chill for a few hours before serving.

Notes

Ground almonds work particularly well with pear.
