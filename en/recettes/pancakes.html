Apple juice pancakes

Very simple pancakes I discovered during a stay in the Chichibu mountains in Japan.

Plant-based recipePersonal archive

Ingredients

  • 500 g wheat flour
  • 1 sachet baking powder
  • About 500 ml apple juice
  • 1 tbsp sunflower oil
  • Rum or Grand Marnier (optional)
  • Toppings of your choice: chocolate, maple syrup, banana, sugar, jam…

Method

  1. Mix the flour and baking powder.
  2. Gradually add the apple juice until you reach the desired consistency. The thicker the batter, the thicker the pancakes and the longer they will need to cook.
  3. Optionally add a little alcohol.
  4. Lightly oil a frying pan with paper towel dipped in sunflower oil and preheat it over medium heat.
  5. Pour in a small ladle of batter and wait about 2 to 3 minutes before flipping the pancake.
  6. Continue cooking, turning as needed, until fully cooked. Very lightly oil the pan between pancakes if necessary.

Notes

By varying the amount of apple juice, you can go from thick pancakes to a batter closer to a crêpe. Pieces of banana can also be added directly while cooking.
